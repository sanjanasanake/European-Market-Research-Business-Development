# 🇱🇻 Latvia Manufacturing Market Entry & Lead Generation Strategy

A full-cycle Business Development project simulating market entry into Latvia for an international recruitment and staffing company — from market research through lead scoring to a client-ready outreach strategy.

## 📌 Project Objective

Identify and qualify potential manufacturing companies in Latvia that could become clients for international recruitment and staffing services, using publicly available market data, job postings, and company information.

This project demonstrates an end-to-end BD workflow: **market research → lead sourcing → objective lead scoring → decision-maker mapping → multi-channel outreach design.**

## 🔍 Methodology

1. **Market Research** — Analyzed Latvia's economy, demographics, and manufacturing sectors (wood processing, metal fabrication, engineering, logistics) to build an evidence-based Ideal Customer Profile.
2. **Lead Sourcing** — Compiled 55 company records from job portals (CV.lv, NVA Latvia, SS.lv, VisiDarbi.lv), company websites, LinkedIn, and business registries (Lursoft, Firmas.lv).
3. **Lead Scoring** — Evaluated every lead across six weighted factors: industry fit, hiring signal strength, digital presence, contact quality, international/export orientation, and company scale.
4. **Prioritization** — Categorized leads as Hot / Warm / Cold and selected a Top 10 shortlist spanning electronics, glass fibre, metal fabrication, automotive components, shipbuilding, and wood panel manufacturing.
5. **Outreach Design** — Built a decision-maker mapping framework and a multi-channel (LinkedIn → Email → Phone) outreach sequence with measurable KPIs.

## 📊 Key Results

- **55 leads analyzed** (53 unique companies after de-duplication)
- **6 Hot leads · 28 Warm leads · 21 Cold leads**
- **Top 10 shortlist** built around companies with strong export orientation and foreign (German, Danish, Swedish) parent-group ownership — a proxy for readiness to engage an international staffing partner
- Average lead score: **55.6 / 100**
